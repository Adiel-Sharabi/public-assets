Rega — Privacy Policy

Rega is a screen-time companion: it pauses for a moment when you open an app you chose to watch, counts the time, and then offers a genuine choice.

Last updated: 4 November 2026

The short version

  • Rega records only the apps you yourself put on your watchlist — never every app on your phone.
  • The app is fully functional without an account. If you never sign in and never turn on the Shabbat setting, Rega makes almost no network requests — see the one exception below.
  • There is no advertising, no ad SDK and no third-party analytics. Nothing about you is sold, and nothing is shared for advertising.
  • If the app crashes, a crash report is sent to Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix it. It holds technical data only — never your apps, your content or your account. Details below.
  • Rega sends anonymous, aggregate product statistics that are not tied to any account. This is part of the app and cannot be switched off — details below.

What Rega collects, and where it goes

WhatWhyWhere it is stored
App activity — which of your chosen watchlist apps were opened, and for how long, plus how you answered Rega's own prompts. To run the pause, the countdown and the daily/weekly goals. This is the feature itself. On your device, in Rega's own database. Read using Android's PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS permission, which you grant explicitly.
Account details — your email address and a user ID. Only if you choose to sign in, so your history can follow you across devices and you can join a circle. Our backend (Supabase). Optional and never required.
Session and prompt records. Cross-device sync, for signed-in adult accounts only. Our backend, in rows only your account can read. Not sent at all if you are not signed in.
Circle membership and aggregate circle state. So a family or group can share a goal together. Aggregate figures only — never a per-member ranking. Our backend. Only if you create or join a circle.
A city identifier (a public geonameid). To look up candle-lighting times, only if you switch the Shabbat setting on. Sent to hebcal.com. No coordinates, no location permission, no account, no device identifier — Rega never requests location access at all.
Anonymous product statistics — counters only: days recorded, days under target, days the plant bloomed, total watched minutes, how prompts and suggestions were answered. Plus a daily health check: whether Rega's permissions are on, whether its background monitor is running, and how many apps are watched (never which). To tell whether the app actually helps people, rather than guessing. Our backend, keyed by a random identifier created on your device. See the section below.
Crash reports — the error and where in Rega's code it happened, the app version, the phone model and Android version, and the device state at that moment (such as free memory), keyed by a random identifier the crash-reporting library creates. To find and fix crashes. It is part of the app and cannot be switched off. Google Firebase Crashlytics, acting on our behalf. Never the names of your apps, anything you wrote, your account, a circle or a child's identity.
Feedback you choose to send — your message, the technical details shown on the form before you send it, and a screenshot of Rega's own screen only if you tick "attach". To answer you and fix what you report. Our backend. Only when you press send. Deleted after 12 months.

About the anonymous statistics

These are disclosed, not optional. They are part of the app and there is no setting that turns them off. We would rather say so plainly than describe a control that does not exist.

What makes them anonymous is structural, not a promise:

  • They are keyed by a random identifier generated on your device for this purpose alone. It is not your account, it is never sent alongside your account, and it links to nothing else we hold.
  • They contain no app names, no timestamps of anything you did, no free text, and no account identifier — only whole-number counters, and yes/no answers about whether Rega itself is working.
  • The app is not able to read this data back. The database permits writing a row and nothing else, so no holder of the app's key — including us, through the app — can retrieve one.

The set of counters is fixed in the app's own automated checks: adding a new field to this report fails our build, so it cannot quietly grow.

What Rega never does

  • It never watches apps you did not put on your watchlist.
  • It never requests location access, reads your contacts, your messages, your photos, or your screen contents.
  • It never blocks an app or locks you out. Every prompt can be dismissed, always.
  • It never reports one family member's activity to another as an individual figure. Shared goals are group totals only, and there are no leaderboards.
  • It contains no advertising and no third-party tracker of any kind.

Children

Rega is built to be kind to younger users, and a child sees the same gentle wording an adult does. This release is not enrolled in Google Play's "Designed for Families" programme and is not directed at children under 13. If a child uses Rega, we recommend a parent sets it up on the device with them.

Security

All network requests use HTTPS. The app declares no exception for unencrypted traffic, so plain HTTP is blocked by Android itself. Android's own automatic backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off for Rega, so your on-device history is not copied into a cloud backup by the operating system.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your device: uninstalling Rega deletes its database and settings.
  • If you signed in, or joined a circle: a copy exists on our backend. Ask us to delete your account and data — that page says exactly what is removed, what is kept, and how long it takes.
  • The anonymous statistics cannot be deleted on request, because there is deliberately nothing linking them to you — no identifier we could search by.

Changes

If what Rega collects changes, this page changes with it and the date at the top is updated.

על הסטטיסטיקה האנונימית

זה מוצהר, לא אופציונלי. זה חלק מהאפליקציה ואין הגדרה שמכבה את זה. עדיף לומר זאת בפירוש מאשר לתאר שליטה שאינה קיימת.

מה שהופך את זה לאנונימי הוא מבני, לא הבטחה:

  • המפתח הוא מזהה אקראי שנוצר במכשיר למטרה הזו בלבד. הוא אינו החשבון, לעולם אינו נשלח לצידו, ואינו מתחבר לשום דבר אחר.
  • אין בו שמות אפליקציות, אין חותמות זמן של פעולות, אין טקסט חופשי ואין מזהה חשבון — רק מונים שלמים, ותשובות כן/לא על כך שרגע עצמו עובד.
  • האפליקציה אינה מסוגלת לקרוא את הנתונים האלה בחזרה. מסד הנתונים מתיר כתיבת שורה בלבד, כך שאף מחזיק מפתח של האפליקציה אינו יכול לשלוף שורה.

אוסף המונים נעול בבדיקות האוטומטיות של האפליקציה: הוספת שדה חדש לדיווח מפילה את הבנייה, כך שהוא אינו יכול לגדול בשקט.

מה Rega לעולם לא עושה

  • לעולם לא עוקב אחרי אפליקציות שלא סומנו לרשימת המעקב.
  • לעולם לא מבקש גישה למיקום, לאנשי קשר, להודעות, לתמונות או לתוכן המסך.

  • לעולם לא מדווח על פעילות של בן משפחה אחד לאחר כנתון אישי. יעדים משותפים הם סכום קבוצתי בלבד, ואין טבלאות דירוג.
  • אין בו פרסומות ואין בו שום גשש של צד שלישי.

ילדים

‏Rega נבנה כדי להיות עדין גם למשתמשים צעירים, וילד רואה בדיוק את אותה שפה רכה שמבוגר רואה. הגרסה הזו אינה רשומה לתוכנית "Designed for Families" של Google Play ואינה מכוונת לילדים מתחת לגיל 13. אם ילד משתמש ב־Rega, מומלץ שהורה יגדיר את זה יחד איתו במכשיר.

אבטחה

כל פנייה לרשת היא ב־HTTPS. האפליקציה אינה מצהירה על חריג לתעבורה לא מוצפנת, ולכן HTTP רגיל נחסם על ידי אנדרואיד עצמו. הגיבוי האוטומטי של אנדרואיד וההעברה בין מכשירים מכובים עבור Rega, כך שההיסטוריה במכשיר אינה מועתקת לגיבוי ענן על ידי מערכת ההפעלה.

  • את הסטטיסטיקה האנונימית אי אפשר למחוק לפי בקשה, כי במכוון אין שום דבר שמקשר אותה לאדם מסוים — אין מזהה שאפשר לחפש לפיו.
